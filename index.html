Research Goal

My goal is to build efficient, extensible, and interactive multimodal AI systems that can perceive, reason, and act across language, vision, audio, and video.

Recent Interests

I am currently interested in omni-modal model orchestration, agentic reinforcement learning, world models, human-computer interaction, long-video understanding, and efficient inference.

Publications Projects Blog CV

Research Interests

Omni-modal orchestration

Training-free coordination of modality experts through LLM routing, persistent memory, and full-duplex interaction.

Long-video reasoning

Event-aware and semantic-boundary-aware frame selection for efficient long-video understanding.

Efficient and adaptive inference

Speculative decoding, online drafting, and lightweight adaptation for faster foundation-model deployment.

Agentic systems and world models

Agentic RL, world-model-style reasoning, and HCI-oriented evaluation for interactive AI systems.
