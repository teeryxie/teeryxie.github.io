Tianyu Xie / 谢天宇

I am a master's student in Artificial Intelligence at the Media Analytics and Computing (MAC) Lab, Xiamen University, advised by Prof. Xiawu Zheng. I joined the lab in September 2025.

My research focuses on omni-modal modeling across language, vision, audio, and video, and full-duplex human–AI interaction, where a system can listen while generating a response and react to interruptions.

I study how to coordinate specialized models without additional alignment training, reuse context across modalities and turns, and evaluate participation in audio-visual dialogue. My work spans method design, model pre-training and post-training, and reproducible inference and evaluation.

Research engineering

I develop training and evaluation pipelines for multimodal systems and have submitted over 4,000 lines of code to SGLang-Omni and vLLM-Omni. My engineering work includes evaluation automation, regression testing, and inference optimization, with code review and validation on GPU systems.

I work with PyTorch, Megatron-LM, DeepSpeed, and LLaMA-Factory for distributed training, supervised fine-tuning, and reinforcement learning, including multi-GPU training on Slurm clusters.
