I am a master's student in Artificial Intelligence at Xiamen University, working with Prof. Xiawu Zheng in the MAC Lab. I also conduct research on pre-training and post-training at a leading AI startup.

My research interests lie in omni-modal modeling and full-duplex human–AI interaction. I focus on real-time multimodal understanding, cross-modal context and memory, and the evaluation of interactive models.

Join Our Group

Our group at Xiamen University, led by Prof. Xiawu Zheng, welcomes students interested in multimodal models and agents, automated machine learning, model compression, and efficient deployment. Visit Prof. Zheng's homepage to learn about our research, recent work, and group members.

To apply, please send your CV to zhengxiawu@xmu.edu.cn and cc teery@xmumac.com and maqingchuan@xmumac.com, along with a brief introduction to your research interests and relevant experience.
