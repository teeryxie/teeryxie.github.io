I'm a master's student in Artificial Intelligence at Xiamen University. I joined the MAC Lab in September 2025 and work with Prof. Xiawu Zheng.

I work on omni-modal models that process language, images, audio, and video, and on full-duplex interaction—systems that can listen while speaking. I'm interested in when an assistant should respond, how it can reuse context across modalities, and how to evaluate these abilities in conversation.

My first-author work includes Training-Free Multimodal Large Language Model Orchestration (ICML 2026) and SocialOmni. Alongside these projects, I work on model training and the code needed to run and evaluate multimodal systems.
