I am Tianyu Xie (谢天宇), a master's student in Artificial Intelligence at Xiamen University, advised by Prof. Xiawu Zheng at the MAC Lab. I joined the lab in September 2025.

My research focuses on omni-modal modeling across language, vision, audio, and video, and full-duplex human–AI interaction, where a system listens while responding and handles interruptions. I study training-free coordination of specialized models, cross-modal memory, and evaluation of participation in audio-visual dialogue.

I am the first author of Training-Free Multimodal Large Language Model Orchestration (ICML 2026) and SocialOmni. My work spans method design, model pre-training and post-training, and inference and evaluation systems.
