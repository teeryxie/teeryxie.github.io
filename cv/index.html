Selected Research

Training-Free Multimodal Large Language Model Orchestration

ICML 2026 · CCF-A · First Author. Training-free omni-modal orchestration with LLM routing, full-duplex streaming speech interaction, and cross-modal memory.

SocialOmni: Benchmarking Audio-Visual Social Interactivity in Omni Models

arXiv 2026 · Under review · First author. Offline evaluation of speaker identification, turn-entry decisions, and response generation using query-time-bounded evidence; 2,000 perception items, 200 interaction-generation items, and 11 evaluated omni models.

Wavelet-based Frame Selection by Detecting Semantic Boundary for Long Video Understanding

CVPR 2026 · CCF-A · Fourth Author. Training-free long-video frame selection with wavelet-based semantic-boundary detection and adaptive frame allocation.

A2RBench: An Automatic Paradigm for Formally Verifiable Abstract Reasoning Benchmark Generation

ICML 2026 · CCF-A · Fourth Author. Automatic generation, augmentation, verification, and analysis for formally grounded abstract reasoning benchmarks.

An Attention Mechanism-Improved YOLOv7 Object Detection Algorithm for Hemp Duck Count Estimation

SCI · JCR Q1 · Second Author. CBAM-enhanced YOLOv7 for dense hemp duck counting and intelligent livestock monitoring.

Duplex-MPE: Benchmarking Multi-Party Interaction in Full-Duplex Dialogue

arXiv 2026 · Co-author. Continuous-audio evaluation of when an assistant should answer, stay silent, or stop speaking in multi-party full-duplex dialogue.

Diagnosing and Mitigating Perception-Decision Misalignment in Omni-LLMs via Modality Subspace Activation

arXiv 2026 · Co-author. Diagnoses whether decisions depend on multimodal evidence and rebalances modality contributions during inference without additional training.

WaveZip: Wavelet-Driven Space-Time Decoupling for Video Token Condensation

arXiv 2026 · Co-author. Training-free video token compression using wavelets to separate temporal redundancy from spatial detail.

Unleashing MLLMs on the Edge: A Unified Framework for Cross-Modal ReID via Adaptive SVD Distillation

arXiv 2026 · Co-author. A unified model for matching identities across images, infrared, sketches, and text, with knowledge distillation for edge deployment.
